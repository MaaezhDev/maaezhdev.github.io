From green bean to your cup in seven days

1Pick

We buy small lots directly from two farms in Colombia and Ethiopia and taste every sample before it ships.

2Roast

Every Tuesday, 12 kilos at a time, light enough that you can still taste where it was grown.

3Pour

Beans rest for three days, then go straight to the grinder. Anything older than three weeks becomes cold brew.

Questions we get a lot

Do you have Wi-Fi and outlets?

Yes to both. On weekends we ask laptop users to share the big table.

Can I buy beans to take home?

Every Wednesday we put 250 g bags of the week's roast on the shelf. We'll grind them for your brewer.

Are you dog friendly?

Very. There's a water bowl by the door and biscuits behind the counter.

Do you cater events?

We bring a pop-up espresso bar for 20 to 150 people. Ask us for a quote.
